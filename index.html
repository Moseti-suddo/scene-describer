My People

People you trust, for when you just want to talk — separate from your emergency contact above.

You can also long-press anywhere on the screen, or say "emergency" or "help me", to send an SOS at any time — from any screen. Say "did they confirm" to check if your contact has responded, or "is my contact confirmed" to check their setup status. Say "I need someone to talk to" to reach someone from your My People list.

Having trouble with the buttons or voice above? Use the plain settings page instead.

Places

You can also say "navigate to", "take me to", or "directions to" followed by a place, from any screen. While a route is playing, say "next", or tap Next, to hear the next step.

Prototype note: this plays back a real walking route step by step. It does not yet track your live position, so steps advance manually rather than automatically as you walk.
