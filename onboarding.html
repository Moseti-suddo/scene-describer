Voice Commands

Double-tap anywhere to talk. Here's what you can say:

"Describe what's around me"
Get a fresh description of your surroundings
"Read this"
Reads any visible text out loud — signs, labels, documents
"Navigate to [place]"
Get real walking directions, spoken step by step
"Emergency" or "Help me"
Sends an SOS to your emergency contact
"Did they confirm?"
Check if your emergency contact responded
"Who's here?"
Recognize registered familiar faces in frame
